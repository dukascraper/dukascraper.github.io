Frequently asked questions
What can I search for?

Anything — a topic, a question, or a few keywords works. You don't need a starting link.

What file types can it read?

Web pages, PDF documents, Word documents, and audio recordings, all in one search.

Does it work in Amharic?

Yes — Amharic and Ge'ez-script content is understood alongside English.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. Type a topic and search — no setup or configuration required.

Is this safe and legal to use?

Use should comply with your local laws and the terms of the sites and sources you search.
